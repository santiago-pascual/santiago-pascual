# Santiago Pascual

Quantitative research, scientific computing and software engineering.

Based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Currently preparing for undergraduate studies in Physics at the University of Buenos Aires (UBA).

My work focuses on the intersection of mathematics, statistics, computer science and financial markets, with an emphasis on empirical research, reproducibility and systematic decision-making.

## Selected Projects

### DÉDALO I — Systematic Futures Research

An independent quantitative research and paper-trading platform for Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 futures.

- Multi-strategy portfolio construction and regime-dependent signals.
- Historical simulation and out-of-sample evaluation.
- Statistical robustness testing and execution modeling.
- Monitored paper-trading infrastructure with risk controls and recovery mechanisms.

The research and execution repository is private. Public research documentation is in preparation.

### [The Machine — Quantitative Equity Research](https://github.com/santiago-pascual/The-Machine-Project)

A quantitative equity research framework combining statistical feature engineering, machine learning, market regime detection and portfolio optimization.

Research areas include Black-Litterman allocation, covariance estimation, meta-labeling, volatility modeling and backtest overfitting.

### [Quant Alpha Agro — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026](https://github.com/santiago-pascual/quant-alpha-agro)

A scientific computing project investigating long-term soil-water trends across the Argentine Pampas using NASA Earth observation datasets.

The research covers 20 years of monthly observations, geospatial data processing, statistical trend estimation and uncertainty quantification.

### [Quantitative Risk Analytics — C++](https://github.com/santiago-pascual/cpp-quant-risk-dashboard)

A dependency-free C++ implementation of financial return statistics, volatility estimation, annualization and compound growth analysis.

### [Monte Carlo Trading Simulator — C++](https://github.com/santiago-pascual/Montecarlo-Simulation)

A stochastic simulation tool for investigating trading expectancy, terminal equity distributions and probabilistic financial outcomes.

## Research Philosophy

I approach quantitative research as an empirical discipline. A model is only as useful as the evidence supporting it, the assumptions underlying it and its ability to generalize beyond the data used to develop it.

My projects emphasize transparent methodology, explicit limitations and the distinction between historical results and forward observations.
